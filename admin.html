What do these terms mean?

Ref (reference ID) – a short code such as TV-D4FDA5 given to every block. The customer sees the same code on their screen, so support can find the case instantly.

Card testing – several failed card payments in a row. Criminals try stolen card numbers on a small payment to see which ones work. Bot attack – payments sent faster than a human can type, which means a script.

Lock level – how many times this account was blocked in 24 hours. Level 1 lasts 15 minutes and the customer may unblock themselves with an emailed code. Level 2 (a repeat) lasts 60 minutes and needs support or waiting. Level 3 lasts 24 hours and is marked MANUAL REVIEW.

Auto-unblocks in – time left before the block lifts by itself. Blocked at – when it started.

Blocked: the account / this card / this browser session – what was frozen. One incident freezes all of them together, so they appear as one row and one Unblock clears them all.

Unblock / Clear All – lifts the block now instead of waiting. Use it for a genuine customer caught by mistake.

Admin action history – a permanent list of every unblock (by an admin or by the customer themselves), kept after the server restarts.
